# 원문 대조 (2026-10-10): Rimal & Real 2003, Smith et al. 2006, Kalny et al. 2026, Lapinski et al. 2013, Shulman et al. 2017

다섯 편 모두 원문 전문을 읽었다 **[전문]**. 쪽수는 출판본 기준이고, Kalny et al.은 원고본(저널·DOI 표기 없음)이다.

## 원문에서 확인한 핵심 사실
- **Rimal & Real 2003** (N = 353, Texas A&M): 모형 = DN, 커뮤니케이션 패턴, IN(social approval, benefits to oneself, benefits to others), 집단정체성(similarity, aspiration). 가설은 네 개념이 각각 소비와 연관된다는 것(p. 189).
  - "outcome expectations"라는 개념은 없다. benefits to oneself는 IN의 하위 차원이다.
  - DN은 다른 변수를 넣으면 유의하지 않았다(p. 196).
  - DN과의 상호작용 중 유의한 것은 social approval × DN(β = −.11)뿐이고, 예측과 반대 방향이었다(p. 197).
  - 커뮤니케이션은 "past 2 weeks, talked with friends or siblings about drinking" 빈도, 자기 시작·상대 시작 2문항으로 쟀다(p. 192). r = .63(p. 193), ΔR² = 32.5%(p. 194).
  - "we have no reason to believe that communication causes risky behavior. Instead, ... communication among like-minded individuals is a principal mechanism through which norms are transmitted"(p. 199).
  - 대안 설명으로 같은 생각을 가진 또래 선택(pp. 199–200), 역인과, 사회연결망이라는 제3변수(p. 200)를 들었다.
  - 규범의 재정의는 "group identity-based codes of conduct that are understood and disseminated through social interaction"이고, 규범은 제재를 전제한다(p. 185). Carey(1989)의 transmission/ritual view를 원용했다(p. 188).
  - 53%는 4B 단계(benefits to oneself)의 총 R²다.
- **Smith et al. 2006**: 세 위도의 believability가 서로 달랐다(H1, H2).
  - 실제 규범(63–64%)이 비개입 위도(60–70%)에 들어갔다.
  - 캠페인 전후 지각·음주가 변했지만, 다른 위도의 메시지와 비교하지 않았고 성숙 효과도 배제하지 못했다(p. 149). 즉 SJT를 제대로 검증하지 않았다.
  - "boundaries of latitudes vary over time, across populations"(p. 150). 실험이 아니라 형성조사와 캠페인 전후 조사다.
- **Kalny et al. 2026**: 연구 99편, r = .34, I² = 98.42%, 예측구간 −.195 to .725. IN은 태도·의도와, DN은 행동과 더 강하게 연관됐다.
  - 준거집단: 친구 r = .28, 가족 r = .16.
  - Hofstede 사후분석(각주 4)에서 네 차원 모두 유의하지 않았고 설명량은 4%였다.
  - 한계로 "dominance of U.S.-centric research", 그리고 집단주의·tight 문화로 일반화하기 어렵다는 점을 들었다.
  - IN의 조절은 "statistical dependence ... not ... causal dependence"다.
- **Lapinski et al. 2013**: Study 2의 손씻기 비율은 low 88%, high 81%, control 70%였다. 메시지 효과는 유의했고 high와 low는 차이가 없었다.
  - 태도는 high와 control 사이만 유의했다(p. 39). 다만 논의(p. 40)는 둘 다 효과가 있었다고 서술한다.
  - 지각 규범 등을 통제해도 메시지 효과가 유지됐다(검정력 저하, p. 37). 저자들은 "cue to action"으로 해석했다(p. 40).
  - privacy 상호작용에서 "data were not consistent"(p. 39)였다. 저자들은 사회적으로 용인되지 않는 행동이나 제재 위협이 필요할 수 있다고 해석했다(p. 41).
  - believability는 low가 high보다 높았다(p. 36). 저자들은 high 통계가 거부 위도에 들었을 가능성을 SJT로 해석했다(p. 42).
  - Cialdini et al. (1990)의 현장실험에서는 무엇이 조작됐는지 불분명했다고 지적했다(p. 23).
- **Shulman et al. 2017**: 821개 연구를 내용분석했다.
  - 이론: TPB 35.4%(p. 1199). "these are not theories about normative influence"(p. 1208).
  - 규범 유형: 주관적 규범 40.8%, 기술적 규범 28.9%, 명령적 규범 18.4%.
  - 준거집단: generic 준거 "nearly a third"(p. 1209), 복수 준거 17.3%.
  - 설계: 설문 76.3%, 횡단 71.8%.
  - 지역: 미국 50.8%(p. 1207). 서구 편중과 집단주의 비교 연구의 부족(p. 1210).
  - TPB 연구 대부분이 motivation to comply를 누락했다(p. 1208).
  - "communication research that focuses on how norms are developed and communicated would be a worthy contribution"(p. 1208).
  - 학문 분야는 코딩하지 않았다.

## 수정 제안 (확정 문안)
**P1-a (필수, TNSB 문단)** 2003 문장 교체:
> Rimal and Real (2003) distinguished descriptive from injunctive norms in a model of normative influence that also included group identity and communication among group members, on the premise that norms are constructed, understood, and disseminated through communication.

(선택 추가) In their survey of college students, descriptive norms were not associated with alcohol consumption once the other variables were entered, and the only significant interaction with descriptive norms ran opposite to the predicted direction.

**P1-b (필수, 위와 연동)** TNSB 첫 검증 문장을 둘로:
> In the theory of normative social behavior (TNSB), Rimal and Real (2005) specified injunctive norms, outcome expectations, and group identity as moderators of the descriptive norm–behavior relationship. In its first test, these variables were directly associated with incoming students’ intentions to drink, and several of them interacted with descriptive norms, although the interaction effects were small relative to the main effects.

**P1-c (필수, 손씻기 63단어 → 2문장, attitudes 삭제)**
> In a field experiment in men’s restrooms, descriptive norm posters increased observed hand washing relative to a no-message control, but the high- and low-prevalence messages did not differ in their effects. The message effects also remained after perceived norms were controlled, which led the authors to interpret the posters as a cue to action rather than as evidence that normative content changed behavior (Lapinski et al., 2013).

**P1-d (필수, "Syntheses have since confirmed" 48단어)**
> A content analysis of 821 studies found that the theory of planned behavior, which is not a theory of normative influence, was the most common framework and that nearly a third of normative referents were generic (Shulman et al., 2017). A later meta-analysis indicated that norm appeals shifted perceived norms reliably, whereas their effects on behavior were small and heterogeneous (Rhodes et al., 2020).

다음 문장 "These syntheses indicate that" → "Thus,".

**P1-e (필수 권장, 문화 A)**
> ..., as in a post hoc analysis in which Hofstede’s dimensions did not explain variation in the descriptive–injunctive norm correlation, although U.S.-centric research dominated the corpus (Kalny et al., 2026).

**P1 선택**:
- 초기 개념 문단 인용에 Rimal & Real (2003)을 추가한다(p. 185).
- 도입 문단 첫 절에 Shulman et al. (2017)을 추가한다.
- D 문단 뒤에 "Of importance, almost all studies that used the subjective norm construct of the theory of planned behavior omitted its motivation to comply component (Shulman et al., 2017)."를 둔다.
- 각주 1의 Perkins & Berkowitz 절을 삭제한다(SNA 각주와 중복).

**P2-a (필수, 차원 1)** 따옴표 없이 원문을 그대로 옮긴 부분이 있다. 최소 수정으로 따옴표와 (p. 199)를 넣고 "found it" → "found them"으로 고친다. 확장안:
> Rimal and Real (2003) treated communication about drinking, indexed by how often students talked about drinking with friends or siblings, as a central concept and found it strongly associated with consumption. Rather than treating this association as causal, they proposed that “communication among like-minded individuals is a principal mechanism through which norms are transmitted” (p. 199).

**P2-b (필수, 차원 2)** p. 42와 거의 같은 문장이다:
> Lapinski et al. (2013) found that participants perceived the prevalence conveyed by each hand washing poster as intended but rated the high-prevalence statistic as less believable than the low-prevalence one.

(선택) The authors suggested that the high-prevalence statistic may have fallen in recipients’ latitude of rejection (Smith et al., 2006).

**P2-c (필수 권장, 차원 3)** privacy 결과가 빠졌다:
> ... tested behavioral privacy in the field, where the data were not consistent with the predicted interaction between the messages and privacy.

(선택) The authors suggested that the interaction may occur only for socially unacceptable behaviors and that the threat of sanctions, which descriptive norm messages do not necessarily imply, may have the strongest effect.

**P2-d (권장, 기여 3)** Smith et al. (2006)은 실험이 아니다. "in the communication experiments" → "in communication research".

**P2 선택(발전)**:
- 차원 4에 Carey 근거를 넣는다: "Rimal and Real (2003) had drawn on Carey’s (1989) transmission and ritual views of communication to argue that communication patterns among group members reinforce group identity."
- 차원 4에 Shulman의 제안을 넣는다: "Shulman et al. (2017) likewise recommended communication research on how norms are developed and communicated."
- 공백(문화)에 근거를 넣는다: "half of the studies in one content analysis were conducted in the United States (Shulman et al., 2017)".
- 공백(채널)에 측정 문제를 넣는다: "... as frequency rather than as content, and with friends and siblings combined".
- 아이디어 1의 대안을 구체화한다: "namely that drinkers seek like-minded peers and that drinking leads students to perceive drinking as widespread".
