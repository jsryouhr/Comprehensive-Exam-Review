# Maria Knight Lapinski 문체 프로파일

분석 자료 (전문 정독):
1. Lapinski, M. K., & Rimal, R. N. (2005). An explication of social norms. *Communication Theory, 15*(2), 127–147. — 이론 설명(explication) 논문, 단독 저술에 가까운 문체가 가장 순수하게 드러남.
2. Lapinski, M. K., Rimal, R. N., DeVries, R., & Lee, E. L. (2007). The role of group orientation and descriptive norms on water conservation attitudes and behaviors. *Health Communication, 22*(2), 133–142. — 실험 논문, 가설·방법·결과·논의의 정형 문장이 드러남.
3. Yoon, H., Jang, Y., Lapinski, M. K., Turner, M. M., Peng, T.-Q., & Lee, S. (2024). The role of collective group orientation and social norms on physical distancing behaviors for disease prevention. *Health Communication.* — 윤형로 1저자, Lapinski 교신저자. 윤형로 초고가 Lapinski의 손을 거쳐 어떻게 바뀌는지를 보여주는 기준점.

## 1. 거시 구조: 논문을 어떻게 설계하는가

**(a) 첫 문단은 "왜 커뮤니케이션 학자가 이 문제를 다뤄야 하는가"로 시작한다.**
통계나 사회문제 나열이 아니라, 개념적 정당화로 연다.
> "The study of norms is of particular importance to communication scholarship because, by definition, norms are social phenomena, and they are propagated among group members through communication (Kincaid, 2004)." (2005)

> "An increasing body of work in the behavior change literature points to the need to focus on behavioral attributes instead of behavioral domains ..." (2007)

**(b) 서론 끝에서 목적을 병렬 동명사 세 개로 선언한다.**
> "The purpose of this article is to explore the intersection between social norms and communication by **specifying** the meaning of norms, **delineating** the moderators ..., and **highlighting** some of the attributes of behaviors ..." (2005)

> "The contribution of this paper is two-fold: 1) to better understand ..., 2) to test and extend ..." (2024)

**(c) 섹션 전환마다 길잡이 문장(signposting)을 넣는다.**
- "Before addressing these issues, however, we discuss several important conceptual distinctions in the literature on norms."
- "In the following sections, we briefly review the moderators ..., and then we extend the model by including ..."
- "Each of these moderators will be discussed in turn in the following section with a particular focus on group orientation, which is the subject of this investigation."
- "a point we shall return to below."
- "The interaction between injunctive and descriptive norms has been discussed above, and it will not be repeated here."

**(d) 개념은 반드시 "대비"로 정의한다.**
collective vs. perceived / descriptive vs. injunctive / group identity vs. group orientation / behavioral attributes vs. behavioral domains / compliance vs. internalization / effect dependence vs. informational dependence.
> "Distinct from the construct of identification with group members (i.e., the extent to which persons see themselves as similar to and strive to be like group members; Rimal & Real, 2005) is the notion of group or collective orientation (i.e., the extent to which group goals prevail over individual goals)." (2007)

> "It is, of course, likely that the two norms would be related, but they are conceptually distinct." (2005)

**(e) 선행연구의 "혼재된 결과(mixed results)"를 출발점으로 삼고, 그 원인을 이론적으로 진단한다.**
> "Empirical support for normative influences is, at best, mixed." / "Despite these results, it would be incorrect to conclude that norms are inconsequential. Rather, given numerous reasons to believe the contrary ..., a more productive strategy would be to determine whether ..." (2005)

> "These mixed findings are due, in part, to methodological challenges inherent in studying social norms and in evaluating interventions ... Moreover, the moderators of the norm–behavior relationship have only recently been specified." (2007)

**(f) 논의(Discussion)의 정해진 순서.**
1. 무엇을 했는지 한 문장 재진술 ("This study examined the role of ...")
2. 조작/기본 효과 확인 → "The more meaningful contribution of this study, however, lies in ..."
3. 핵심 상호작용의 **패턴을 말로 풀어 설명** (기울기 방향, 어느 수준에서 강한지)
4. 이론적 함의: TNSB의 경계 조건(boundary condition)·개념 명료화 요구
5. 캠페인·개입 설계 함의 ("norms-based communication interventions need to target their messages according to this variable")
6. 한계: "Although this study was carefully conducted, it is not without limitations; some of which are inherent in the study design. First, ... Second, ... Finally, our study design precludes causal claims"
7. 결론: "In conclusion, it appears that ..." / "In summary, this paper extends ..."

## 2. 문단·문장 구조

**계량 지표 (pdftotext 추출 후 본문 기준, 표·주석 일부 포함):**

| 지표 | 2005 (Comm Theory) | 2007 (Health Comm) |
|---|---|---|
| 평균 문장 길이 | 30.0 단어 | 31.7 단어 |
| 문두 "Thus" | 13회 | 10회 |
| 문두 "That is" | 0회 | 5회 |
| 문두 Additionally / Furthermore | 0회 / 0회 | 0회 / 0회 |
| 문두 "Moreover" | 2회 | 2회 |
| "we" / "our" | 16회 / 4회 | 10회 / 4회 |
| 문두 "As"(because 용법) | 거의 없음 | 거의 없음 |
| "Because" | 4회 | 2회 |

즉, Lapinski의 문장도 짧지 않다(평균 30 단어 안팎). 차이는 길이가 아니라 **균형**이다: 긴 문장은 em-dash·괄호 삽입구로 구조가 보이고, 한 문장이 한 주장만 담는다. 접속은 거의 "Thus"와 "That is"로 하며, Additionally/Furthermore는 쓰지 않는다.

- **문단 첫 문장은 주장(claim)이다.** 인용으로 시작하지 않는다. 그 다음 근거·예시·단서(qualification) 순.
- **한 문단 = 한 가지 일.** 2024 출판본의 문단은 윤형로 초고보다 눈에 띄게 짧다.
- 문장은 길어도 **균형 잡힌 복문**이다. 삽입구는 em-dash(—)나 괄호로 처리하고, 콤마 연쇄로 늘어뜨리지 않는다.
  > "Rimal and Real (in press) identified three such moderators—injunctive norms, outcome expectations, and group identity—that influence behaviors directly and also through their interaction with descriptive norms."
- **대조 구문**을 즐겨 쓴다: "on the one hand ... on the other hand", "whereas", "as compared to", "Conversely", "Likewise", "By the same token".
- **"Thus, X is a facilitator of, but not a necessary condition for, Y"** 식의 정밀한 한정이 특징.
  > "Thus, though closely related, ambiguity and novelty are not synonymous."
  > "Granted, individuals may feel pressure from their partners to use a condom, and this pressure can substantially influence behavior ..., but such an influence is not normative; it is interpersonal."
- 수사적 질문을 설명 논문에서 사용한다 (2005): "How is normative information shared among group members? What factors must exist in order for people to exercise their own judgment and defy normative influences? Conversely, what factors promote the influence of norms?"
- 열거는 (a) (b) (c) 또는 First / Second / Finally. 결론에서는 "(a) ...; (b) ...; (c) ...; and (d) ..." 로 기여를 묶는다.

## 3. 어조(Tone)

- **자신감 있되 과장하지 않음.** 주장의 크기를 증거의 크기에 맞춘다. 횡단 자료는 "associated with", 실험은 "resulted in".
- **1인칭 복수 "we/our"를 자연스럽게 사용** ("we propose", "we suspect", "we deemed this acceptable because", "It is our premise here that", "Our data indicate"). 수동태·"the current study" 일변도가 아니다.
- **단서(caveat)를 숨기지 않고 앞세운다.** "However, the effect size for both moderations was small and the findings should be interpreted with this caveat in mind."
- **이론 중심.** 모든 발견은 TNSB를 확장·도전·명료화하는 것으로 귀결된다. "Our findings have significant theoretical implications as they challenge the boundaries of the TNSB."
- **실무 함의로 마무리.** "formative research", "campaign design", "target/tailor messages".
- 다른 학자를 비판할 때도 공손하고 건설적: "What is often neglected in this literature is the question of ..." / "Although the exact mechanism underlying this effect has yet to be determined, it is possible that ..."

## 4. 시그니처 어휘·표현

### 동사
delineate, explicate, specify, proffer, posit, construe, pertain to, enact (a behavior), tap into, confer (benefits), obviate, exercise (an influence), induce (perceptions), modify (perceptions), lend credence to, highlight, address, articulate (as articulated in the TRA), subsume ("will be subsumed by an interaction"), supersede ("was superseded by"), probe (an interaction), warrant

### 명사구
normative influence(s), the norm–behavior relationship, the descriptive norm–behavior relationship, prevalence perceptions / perceived prevalence, referent group / referent others, one's social midst, social sanctions, codes of conduct, heuristic cue / decisional shortcut, social proof, normative restructuring (strategies), norms-based approaches / interventions / campaigns, behavioral attributes, boundary condition, the purview of the TNSB, cultural dynamics, formative research, the behavior in question, a paucity of research, mounting evidence, mixed results

### 부사·접속 표현
Thus, / Hence, / That is, / In other words, / Put simply, / Of importance, / Importantly, / Notable in ... is / It should also be noted that / It is important to note that / Of course, / Granted, / Yet, / Further, / Likewise, / Conversely, / By the same token / Taken as a whole, / everything else being equal / ceteris paribus / albeit / notwithstanding / in part / at best / at a minimum / to begin with / in turn

### 추론·단서 표현 (hedging의 표준형)
- "It is likely that, everything else being equal, ..."
- "It may well be that ..." / "It may be that ..."
- "it is possible that ..." / "it appears that ..." / "we suspect"
- "This suggests a potential limitation to the scope of the TNSB—that its predictions may not hold for behaviors where ..."

### 사람을 가리키는 말
persons, people, individuals, participants. (he/she, his/her 사용하지 않음.)

### 표기 관행
- "norms-based" (s 포함) — approaches, interventions, campaigns 모두.
- "the TNSB" — 관사 포함. "The TNSB predicts ...", "predictions of the TNSB".
- "descriptive norm–behavior relationship" — en-dash 연결.
- 인용 리뷰 지시: "(see Borsari & Carey, 2001, 2003, for reviews)", "(for a review, see Byron et al., 2016)" 둘 다 사용. "c.f." 혹은 "cf." 는 "비교하라" 뜻으로만.
- 괄호 안 인용은 보통 2~4편. 10편 넘게 나열하지 않는다.

## 5. 정형 문장(Templates): 가설·방법·결과

### 가설
- 직접효과: "H1: Perceived descriptive norms will be positively associated with physical distancing behavior."
- 조절효과: "H3: Perceived injunctive norms will moderate the perceived descriptive norm–physical distancing behavior relationship **such that** the relationship will become stronger as perceived injunctive norms increase."
- 2007년식 비교형: "H4a: The influence of perceived prevalence on attitudes will be greater when group orientation is higher than when group orientation is lower."
- 가설 도입구: "Thus, the following are predicted:" / "Thus, the third hypothesis predicts:" / "The following hypothesis is advanced:" / "In line with this premise, the following is hypothesized:"

### 측정(Measures)
- "The extent to which individuals are oriented toward group as opposed to individual goals was measured with a series of items derived from previous measures (Triandis, 1995). The CFA yielded a 5-item unidimensional solution (α = .73) including items such as: '...' and '...'. Higher numbers indicate stronger orientation toward the group (M = 4.78, SD = .97)."
- "**Perceived descriptive norms** were measured with two questions (e.g., ...) on a 0–100 scale. A composite score ... was calculated by averaging the scores of the two questions (M = 65.46, SD = 24.98, r = .84, p < .01)."
- "All scales in the survey were developed using adapted versions of measures for which prior literature ... has demonstrated reliability and validity evidence."
- "When measures contained at least four items, confirmatory factor analyses (CFA) were conducted based on *a priori* measurement models prior to assessing scale reliability."

### 결과
- **"the data were consistent with H1"** / **"the data were not consistent with H7"** — 절대 "H1 was supported/confirmed/proved"라고 쓰지 않는다.
- "Thus, the data were consistent with the first hypothesis and indicated that perception of descriptive norms can be induced independently of perceptions of injunctive norms."
- "The interaction term was not significant (B = .001, β = .02, t = 2.07, p = .039); thus, the data was not consistent with H3."
- "However, the data were not consistent with the direction of the relationship predicted in H4."
- 통계 표기: (M = 5.06, SD = 1.56), t(70) = 8.54, p = .01, r = .71; (R = .50, ΔR² = .08, p = .01); β 와 B 구분; "Notable in the regression models is the strong association of our study covariates with ..."

### 논의·한계
- "By experimentally manipulating descriptive norms, ..., we found that ... Further, lending credence to the conceptual distinction ..., we also found that ..."
- "Among people with a strong group orientation, we found that descriptive norms did not exercise an impact on their attitudes or behaviors; ..."
- "We deemed this acceptable in this case because we were primarily interested in controlling for the effects of this variable, not in testing its influence on our dependent measures."
- "Despite the modest size of these interactive relationships, these findings contribute some insight into ..."
- "Finally, our study design precludes causal claims; we only provide evidence of association"

## 6. Lapinski가 하지 않는 것 (교정 시 기준)

- 문두 "As ..."를 because 뜻으로 쓰지 않는다 → "Because".
- "it is construed that", "it can be inferred that" 같은 비인칭 추론 구문을 쓰지 않는다.
- voluminous, paramount, bedrock, imbue, bifurcated 같은 장식적 어휘를 쓰지 않는다. 격식은 있되 담백하다.
- 문단마다 Additionally / Moreover / Furthermore로 시작하지 않는다. 논리 관계(Thus, Yet, However, Further, Of importance)로 잇는다.
- 하나의 괄호에 인용 10편을 넣지 않는다.
- 통계·유병률 수치를 서론에 길게 늘어놓지 않는다 (한두 개로 문제의 크기만 보여준다).
- "supported the hypothesis"라고 쓰지 않는다.
- 내용 없는 메타 문장("This methodological approach aims to address the complexity of ...")을 쓰지 않는다.
